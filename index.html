Interactive Confidence Calibration Tool

An AI model has drawn a box around everything it thinks might be a flax seed. Your job is to choose how confident the model must be before a box is shown.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter your first and last name.
  2. Find the flax seeds: they are the smooth, glossy, reddish-brown seeds (example on the right). The green seeds are pumpkin and the speckled seeds are chia.
  3. Adjust the slider until only the flax seeds are boxed. Moving it right hides boxes the model is less sure about. Near the top of the range, use the arrow keys on your keyboard for small steps.
  4. When you are happy with what you see, click Save this as my preferred threshold.

Each box is a detection labelled with the model's confidence that the object is a flax seed.
